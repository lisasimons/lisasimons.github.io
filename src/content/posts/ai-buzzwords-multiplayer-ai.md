AI Buzzwords: Multiplayer AI, shared agents, projects, human-agent teams.

One thing I like about AI is it challenges us to question what makes us human. 

Shared agents are essentially like a new team member. A new person that we build ourselves.

Looking into multiplayer agents raises questions for me:
1) How good are we going to be at sharing? 
* What are we sharing - memory, context, credentials, tasks? 
* What are we not sharing? 

2) How willing will people be to change their "ways of working"?
* What will it take to convince (or force) people to embed SHARED agent deliverables into their practices?

3) How useful is shared (possibly generic) information? It's the old centralised vs decentralised debate - bespoke vs efficiencies of scale.
* Tris J Burns  [https://www.linkedin.com/in/tristanjburns] post about dashboards got me thinking about how often communication fails when it doesn't give people the final answer they need, but instead only part of the answer and a whole lot of unnecessary info too - i.e. don't bring me problems, bring me solutions.

4) Who is responsible for the agents that get built? Do we trust someone else to build an agent that our jobs are dependent on? How can we take responsibility for using shared agent deliverables?
